Tajweed

Quran With Tajweed

Focused instruction on accurate Quran recitation according to established Tajweed rules. Students learn precise letter articulation points (Makharij), rules of Noon Sakin, Meem Sakin, Madd, and rhythm.

Suitable for: Children, Youth, Adults & Parents
Foundation

Noorani Qaida

The essential stepping stone for reading the Holy Quran. Beginners master individual Arabic letters, compound letters, short vowels (Harakat), Tanween, Sukoon, and basic joining rules.

Suitable for: Beginners of all ages & young kids
General Knowledge

Islamic Malumaat

Broadens fundamental Islamic knowledge, including five pillars of Islam, articles of faith (Iman), Islamic calendar months, basic Fiqh etiquettes, and fundamental Islamic history.

Suitable for: All learners seeking general Deeni knowledge
Hadith

40 Hadees

Study and memorize 40 concise Hadiths of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ). Focuses on practical advice regarding truthfulness, respect for parents, kindness, patience, and good character.

Suitable for: Children, Teenagers & Adults
Daily Duas

Masnoon Dua

Memorization and understanding of authentic daily supplications for various occasions—such as before meals, after eating, sleeping, waking up, entering/leaving home, and seeking protection.

Suitable for: Children & Adult learners
Prophetic Manners

Sunnate

Practical learning of the noble Sunnah etiquettes of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ). Covers daily habits, eating manners, speech etiquettes, sleeping routines, and social interactions.

Suitable for: Families, Youth & Adults
Seerah

Sirat-un-Nabi

In-depth study of the life of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), from early blessed childhood, prophethood, Mecca & Medina eras, noble attributes, to timeless moral lessons for modern life.

Suitable for: Students, Parents & Adults
