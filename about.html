Our Academy Story

Accessible Islamic Education For Every Home

Fatima Academy is an online educational platform committed to delivering structured, high-quality, and accessible Islamic knowledge to students of all ages across the globe.

We understand that busy modern schedules, geographic constraints, or lack of local centers often make it difficult for families to learn foundational Islamic subjects. Fatima Academy bridges this gap by bringing direct interactive classes into your living room.

With no age limit, our classes are uniquely designed for children building their first Arabic letter skills, teenagers seeking authentic moral grounding, and adult learners perfecting their Quranic recitation.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

✦

Our Mission

To make foundational Deeni knowledge accessible to every Muslim household by utilizing modern online learning tools while maintaining traditional respect, clarity, and authenticity in teaching.

Our Vision

To inspire a confident generation of learners who recite the Quran with accurate Tajweed, embody the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), and practice essential daily Islamic manners with clarity.

Our Learning Approach

Step-by-step guidance tailored to each individual learner. We emphasize patience, clear letter pronunciation (Makharij), practical understanding, and regular practice.

Comprehensive Curriculum

Subjects Offered at Fatima Academy

✦

A balanced curriculum covering Quranic recitation and practical Islamic knowledge.

1. Quran With Tajweed

Recitation rules, correct articulation points, and fluent reading.

2. Noorani Qaida

Arabic alphabet recognition, vowel marks, and foundational reading.

3. Islamic Malumaat

General Islamic knowledge, faith basics, and core history.

4. 40 Hadees

Selected short Hadiths focusing on morals and good conduct.

5. Masnoon Dua

Essential daily supplications for everyday activities.

6. Sunnate

Prophetic traditions, manners, and daily etiquettes.

7. Sirat-un-Nabi

Inspirational life study of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ).
