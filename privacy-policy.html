Notice for Website Owner: This Privacy Policy is a standard baseline policy for static educational websites. Please review and customize these terms to ensure full alignment with your academy's specific operating policies and local data protection regulations before official launch.

At Fatima Academy (accessible via fatimaacademy.in), accessible Deeni education is our primary mission, and safeguarding the privacy of our visitors and students is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we may collect and how we utilize it.

1. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact Fatima Academy via WhatsApp or fill out our online contact form, you voluntarily provide contact information such as:

  • Your Name
  • Mobile / WhatsApp Phone Number
  • Email Address (optional)
  • Subject or Course interest
  • Messages or inquiries regarding class schedules

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you voluntarily submit is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries regarding online Islamic classes
  • To coordinate class timings and provide enrollment details
  • To communicate academy updates or schedule adjustments
  • To deliver customer support and improve learner experience

3. Third-Party Services & WhatsApp Communication

Our website utilizes direct WhatsApp messaging integration for user convenience. When you click WhatsApp buttons or submit our contact form, you are redirected to WhatsApp (a service operated by Meta Platforms Inc.). Your interaction on WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

4. Cookies & Website Analytics

This static website does not mandate account creation or store sensitive user profiling cookies. Standard web server logs may automatically record non-personal technical data (such as browser type, IP address, and page views) strictly for performance optimization and site security.

5. Data Security

Fatima Academy takes reasonable technical and organizational precautions to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of student contact information. We do not sell, rent, or trade student contact details to third-party commercial marketers.

6. User Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal contact information you have shared with us, or request deletion of your contact records at any time by messaging us directly on WhatsApp.
